How demand is supplied

Electricity demand continues even when local renewable generation is low, so storage or the wider utility grid supplies the difference.

Worldwide data is requested on demand rather than stored inside the page. Live weather is loaded for the exact selected coordinates. Solar and wind resource conditions are combined with the simulator configuration, while grid carbon intensity and national solar/wind context use the latest available country-level indicators. Battery SOC is simulated inside a controlled 55–75% reserve band and is not live BMS telemetry.
